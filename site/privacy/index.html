SITE INFORMATION

Privacy

A plain-language summary of what this site collects and which outside services may receive information.

Contact messages

When you use a Follincraft contact form, the name, email address, message, and any other information you choose to provide are submitted through Formspree so Chris Follin can receive and respond to the message. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through these forms.

Browser storage

Some interactive tools may store preferences, work-in-progress values, or cart information in your browser. That information normally remains on your device unless a tool clearly provides a separate sharing or submission action.

Embedded and third-party services

Pages may load Google Fonts, embedded Google Maps, Formspree forms, product retailers, social networks, or other linked services. Those providers may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, browser type, and the referring page, subject to their own privacy terms.

How information is used

Contact information is used to answer inquiries, discuss possible work, and maintain the resulting correspondence. Follincraft does not sell contact-form information. Service providers may retain information according to their own policies and operational requirements.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting a contact form, clear local browser storage through your browser settings, and open third-party links only when you choose. To ask about a message you previously submitted, email cfollin84@gmail.com.

Fabricator + designer + manager

I understand the work from the quote to the shop floor.

I manage custom fabrication at Empire Metal Products in Phoenix. I started there as a fabricator and welder in 2005, left to run CNC equipment at PMA Photo Metals, and was called back in 2010 to take over custom fabrication.

I now manage the customer, design, material, people, schedule, and production decisions behind one-off products, production parts, prototypes, OEM work, and specialty fabrication.

I can talk to the customer, draw the work, plan the material, manage the people, troubleshoot production, and get the job out the door.

Caframo Sirocco II Fan

A proper 12V cabin fan for vehicle sleeping when cheap clip fans stop being cute and start being annoying.

Overview

Small air movement can change whether a vehicle sleep setup feels livable or stale.

Heat changes how well you sleep, how long you stay inside the vehicle, and whether camp feels workable or miserable once the sun drops. A real 12V fan matters because it moves meaningful air, mounts cleanly, and feels like part of the setup instead of another little workaround hanging from a strap.

The Sirocco has the reputation it has for a reason. It folds away neatly, draws little enough power to make sense in an actual vehicle setup, and moves enough air to justify the price in a way generic fans usually do not.

This is one of those comfort items that stops feeling optional the minute you spend a hot night in a van, camper shell, trailer, or tiny cabin and realize airflow is the whole game.

Best for Van beds, truck campers, hot sleepers, humid nights, and builds that need directed low-draw airflow.
Not for People who only need a throwaway tent fan or do not have a clean 12V mounting spot.

If you sleep inside a vehicle in warm weather, real airflow is not luxury. It is the difference between rest and endurance.

My Notes

This is the fan I would use when airflow needs to be part of the build, not another loose object sliding around at night.

  • Mount it where it can hit the sleeping position without becoming head-bump hardware.
  • Put it on a fused 12V circuit instead of hacking together temporary wiring.
  • Think about night use: noise, switch access, and where the fan points when you are half asleep.

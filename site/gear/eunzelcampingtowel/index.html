Overview

A fast-drying towel is boring until a wet cotton towel is hanging in the van for a day.

Microfiber towels pack smaller and dry faster than regular bath towels, which matters when storage is limited and airflow is not guaranteed.

For showers, swimming, dish spills, and general camp cleanup, a dedicated quick-dry towel keeps wet fabric from taking over the vehicle.

The tradeoff is feel. Some people hate microfiber. If that is you, buy a better travel towel or accept the bulk of cotton.

Best forVan showers, swim stops, travel kits, gym bags, compact hygiene bins, and people who need towels to dry fast.
Not forPeople who hate microfiber feel or want a plush home towel experience.

This is low-glamour gear that makes tight spaces easier to live in. The whole point is getting it dry fast.

  • Hang it immediately, even if you are tired.

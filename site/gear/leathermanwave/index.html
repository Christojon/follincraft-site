Overview

This is the multitool I would rather have nearby than talk about. It earns its place by being useful before a small problem turns into a bigger one.

The Wave+ is not the flashiest thing in the kit. It is the one you keep reaching for when something needs to be cut, tightened, snipped, adjusted, or persuaded back into place. Full-size pliers, usable blades, real drivers, and a file cover most of the random problems that show up around camp, on the road, and in the garage.

The stainless build handles normal sweat, dirt, and the occasional splash. It is not a delicate showpiece. It likes living in glove boxes, camp drawers, and dusty center consoles. Flip out outside blades mean you can get to a knife without unfolding the whole tool, which is the kind of small detail you appreciate when your hands are cold or wet.

If you only want one multitool that feels like an actual piece of kit instead of a novelty keychain, this is the one to beat.

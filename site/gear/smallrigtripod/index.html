Overview

A tripod is annoying until you need the shot to stop moving.

The SmallRig 71 inch tripod is tall enough for eye level shots even if you are not short, but still folds down small enough to ride in a camp bin or behind a truck seat. The legs are simple flip locks, the ball head just works, and it feels a lot more solid than the cheap tripods that flex when you bump the camera.

It turns into a monopod when you want something lighter to move around with, and you can flip the center column for low angle shots at ground level. It is not a featherweight carbon stick, but for camp, truck, and general outdoor use the extra stability is worth a little more weight.

For the price, it is an easy upgrade from the wobbly starter tripods, especially if you are starting to trust it with a nicer camera or longer lens.

Best for Camp video, product shots, self-filming, low-light photos, and vehicle-based camera kits.
Not for Backpacking, high-wind professional work, or people who will never stop to set up a shot.

At a Glance
Height
Adjustable from about 16 inches up to roughly 71 inches - goes from low angle shots to full standing height.
Weight and capacity
Around 3.3 lb with a rated load up to around 33 lb - plenty for a mirrorless body and normal lenses.
Head
360 degree ball head for quick framing - lock it down once and it stays put without drooping.
Tripod / Monopod
One leg converts to a monopod for lighter carry when you do not want the full tripod footprint.
Packed size
Folds down to under 17 inches so it fits in most camp bins, duffels, or side compartments.
Extras
Extra 1/4 inch threaded points on the collar for mounting a phone clamp, light, or small monitor.
My Notes

This is not something I would carry everywhere. It is something I want available when the shot matters and the truck is nearby.

  • Keep a quick-release plate with the camera or the tripod becomes decoration.
  • Weight it or lower it when wind picks up.
  • Use it for product photos and self-filming where handheld gets sloppy.
